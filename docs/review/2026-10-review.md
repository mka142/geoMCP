# geomcp review, October 2026

Scope: the whole repository at commit `dd2d004`, before its first push, reviewed on branch `review/fable`.
Priorities, in the order the owner set them: HTTP security, secrets and personal data, correctness of numbers, robustness, tool design, tests, Docker and deploy, code quality.
Baseline before changes: 112 tests passed, pyright and ruff clean.
After the changes: 131 tests passed (10 expected failures for later phases), pyright and ruff clean, the Docker image builds and answers a tool call.

Live calls made during the review, all sparingly: one Sentinel Hub Statistical request over a 1 km circle (0.7 processing units), one request to the PIG-PIB landslide service, the re-recording of the two constraints cassettes (keyless Polish services), and two runs of the example generator.

## Severity scale

- Critical: data or credentials reachable by people outside the allowlist, or a number wrong for every user.
- High: a wrong or misleading number in a common question, or a silent failure that reads as an answer.
- Medium: a robustness or hygiene problem with a realistic trigger.
- Low: cosmetic, speculative, or cheap to live with.

## Critical

None found.

## High

### H1. Flood shares counted rivers, lakes, embankments, labels and the sheet grid

`packages/core/src/geomcp_core/constraints.py:231` (before the fix) requested every queryable layer of each ISOK scenario service and counted every coloured pixel as flooded.
The capabilities list, saved in the Kozanów cassette, shows what that included: rivers and canals, all surface waters, embankments, river kilometrage, crest and water-level labels, and the sheet index, which has no scale limit and draws grid lines on every tile.
Failure scenario: a gmina with gravel-pit lakes and no floodplain gets a non-zero share in every scenario, and every area gets a few grid-line pixels.
Measured on Kozanów (a real floodplain): 8/24/41/22 percent before, 4/19/37/16 percent after, for the 10 percent, 1 percent, 0.2 percent and embankment-failure scenarios.
Fix: draw only the water-depth and hazard-zone layers, selected by the ids in their abstracts, with river and sea variants, and refuse a service that lists neither.
Fixed in a102da8, with three unit tests on the layer selection and the two constraints cassettes re-recorded.

### H2. The cloud-free share depended on the polygon's shape

`packages/core/src/geomcp_core/adapters/sentinel_hub.py:150` computed `valid_fraction` as one minus the no-data share of `sampleCount`.
Sentinel Hub computes statistics on the bounding-box grid and counts pixels outside the polygon as no-data, so the share was the cloud-free share times the polygon's fill of its box.
Checked live: a 1 km circle in a clear July 2025 peaked at 0.83 (fill 0.785); gmina Kąty Wrocławskie fills 0.51 of its box; even the golden square fills 0.95 because the UTM grid is rotated 1.5 degrees here.
Failure scenario: the compare example reported 0.375 and 0.398 for two gminas in summer, half the truth, and the 20 percent `min_valid` threshold dropped acquisitions over irregular areas that were 30 to 40 percent clear.
Fix: divide by the polygon's fill at read time, clamped to 1, so cached chunks stay valid and no quota is spent.
Fixed in db0bb3a, with two unit tests; the compare example now reads 0.573 and 0.678.

### H3. Traffic change since 2020/21 compared different road sections

`packages/core/src/geomcp_core/traffic.py:120` (before the fix) joined the 2020/21 count by equal section number.
Of 2,071 numbers present in both survey files, 771 name a different section or even a different road in 2020/21 (91501 is an A1 section in 2025 and a DK1 section in 2020/21).
Failure scenario: a user asks how traffic changed on a national road and gets a percentage computed against another road's count.
The sections list carries `Porównanie_z_2020`, the comparable 2020/21 section or empty.
Fix: join through that column and report `null` where GDDKiA names no counterpart; the vehicle-category column order was cross-checked against network totals and is right.
Fixed in 55fa20c (cache key v3), with a unit test on a renumbered section.

### H4. Landslides were reported as absent everywhere in Poland

`packages/core/src/geomcp_core/constraints.py:188` (before the fix) read `payload.get("features", [])` from the PIG-PIB SOPO layers.
That service answers every query with HTTP 200 and `{"error": "Requested operation is not supported by this service"}`, both in the cassettes recorded on 9 October and live on 11 October.
The empty list became share 0 and count 0, and the text summary said "None found: Landslides (SOPO); Areas threatened by mass movement".
Failure scenario: a risk profile for a Carpathian gmina declares no landslides.
Fix: an ArcGIS error body, a non-JSON answer, `exceededTransferLimit`, or a GDOŚ page filled to its 200-feature cap becomes a named gap; each source group reports its own gaps so mining and ground motion still answer; a result with gaps is cached for a day together with its gaps, a complete one for 30 days.
Fixed in d1fc648 and 27649a7, with two unit tests; the same check was added to CORINE.
The 200-feature cap applies to GDOŚ pages only; ArcGIS layers flag truncation themselves.
Open question Q1 below: landslides need another access path.

### H5. A partial Sentinel Hub answer was cached forever

`packages/core/src/geomcp_core/adapters/sentinel_hub.py:143` skipped intervals without `outputs` and the chunk was then stored with no expiry when the year was settled.
The recorded 2024 chunk of the golden area is such an answer: `status: PARTIAL` with two days that timed out on Sentinel Hub's side.
Failure scenario: two missing acquisitions in 2024 stay missing for every future question about that area, and a yearly mean is computed from fewer days with no trace.
Fix: a chunk whose answer is not `status: OK` is cached with the 6 hour TTL and logged.
Fixed in b13f4ac, with a unit test that reads the cache file's expiry.

## Medium

### M1. GEOMCP_ALLOWED_HOSTS was a documented no-op

`packages/server/src/geomcp_server/cli.py:168` passed `allowed_hosts` to FastMCP, which ignores it unless its Host guard is enabled; the guard defaults to off.
Verified by starting the server with the setting and sending `Host: evil.example`: the request reached authentication (401) instead of being refused.
Fix: the guard is on only when a list is set (other names get 421) and off otherwise, so nothing changes for a deployment without the setting.
The README and `.env.example` say so.
Fixed in f5b1259 and 27649a7, with a transport test; verified by hand with and without the list.
Question Q6 asks whether FastMCP's `auto` mode (Host checks for loopback binds) should be turned on as well.

### M2. Summary sources ran one after another

`packages/core/src/geomcp_core/service.py:305` queried static sources sequentially, then each dynamic variable sequentially, while `estimate_summary_s` took the maximum of their estimates.
Failure scenario: the `site_profile` prompt asks for about 17 variables from 8 sources; five slow sources at 15 to 25 seconds each take about 100 seconds under a 25 second estimate, so the call runs synchronously and exceeds the 45 second budget and the client's patience.
Fix: all sources run in one thread pool, as `get_timeseries` already did.
Fixed in 995719a, with a timing test using four slow fake sources.

### M3. Voivodeship roads listed as national roads

`packages/core/src/geomcp_core/traffic.py:36` accepted one to three digit refs, so a secondary road with ref 347 appeared in `roads_in_area` and the text said "national roads A4, 346, 347, 5".
Fix: national roads are numbered 1 to 99; three-digit numbers are voivodeship roads.
Fixed in 55fa20c and 519918e (the parsed road lists were cached for 30 days under the old key).

### M4. Processing units attributed to the previous call

`packages/server/src/geomcp_server/app.py:236` charged units to the client's latest logged call, but the running call's own row is written in `finally`, after the charge.
Failure scenario: `geomcp-admin stats` shows the units of a `get_timeseries` call on the `resolve_area` row before it, and the first call of a key is never charged.
Fix: the running call accumulates its cost in a context variable and logs it with its row; background jobs still charge the hand-off call.
Fixed in fc02cc4, with a contract test.

### M5. A test that fails every December

`tests/unit/test_service.py:63` asserted that December of the current year is in the future.
Fixed in fc02cc4: the asserted period is June of next year.

### M6. Cassettes kept re-identifying links, bank accounts and a session id

`tests/recorded/conftest.py:21` redacted names and addresses of sole traders but kept `id` and `link`, which open the person's public CEIDG record.
The VAT whitelist answer for the company example kept 230 bank account numbers, which geomcp never uses.
The REGON cassette kept the test service's session id in request headers and in the login body despite `sid` being in the header filter.
Fix: the scrubbing covers these fields and the login body, drops the header in `before_record_request`, and was applied in place to the three cassettes; no re-recording.
Fixed in 09e128f.

### M7. A crashed job echoed the raw exception to the client

`packages/server/src/geomcp_server/jobs.py:67` put `str(e)` into the client-visible error, while `_run_parallel` deliberately reports only the exception class.
Fixed in fc02cc4: the client sees the class and a hint, the log keeps the traceback.

## Low

### L1. Jobs are not bound to the caller

`packages/server/src/geomcp_server/app.py:734`: any authenticated key can read any job result by id.
Ids are 64 random bits and the team is four trusted people (ADR 0005), so this is acceptable today.
Open: a product decision if the team grows; the job row would need the client name and `get_job` a check.

### L2. Google sign-in accepts any client redirect URI

`packages/server/src/geomcp_server/auth.py:110` leaves FastMCP's `allowed_client_redirect_uris` at its default (all URIs), so any dynamically registered client may name any redirect URI; the consent page is the only guard.
Open: question Q2, because restricting the list may break a client the team uses.

### L3. The Nominatim User-Agent carries a placeholder URL

`packages/core/src/geomcp_core/config.py:6` identifies geomcp with `+https://github.com/`.
Nominatim's usage policy asks for a way to contact the operator.
Open: question Q3, the owner must choose the contact.

### L4. IMGW warning expiry compared in UTC against local timestamps

`packages/core/src/geomcp_core/constraints.py:480` compares `obowiazuje_do` with the current UTC time as strings; IMGW's timestamps look like Polish local time.
Effect: a warning that ended within the last one or two hours still shows as active.
Open: not changed, because the time zone of the feed is not documented and the effect is small.

### L5. Settled weather periods with missing days are reported as final

`packages/core/src/geomcp_core/adapters/cds.py:253`: a sum over a period with a data gap is a lower total; `valid_fraction` carries the share of days present, but the status stays `final` and the text does not mention it.
Open: whether an incomplete settled period should be `preliminary` is a product decision (question Q4).

### L6. Coverage dates of GIOŚ and OpenStreetMap say 2026

`packages/core/src/geomcp_core/adapters/gios.py:148` and `adapters/osm.py:77` declare coverage from 2026-01-01, so the `geomcp://sources` resource says history starts this year while the air-quality history goes back to 2000.
Open: cosmetic; the catalogue entries in `catalog.py` are right.

### L7. The CI Docker smoke test created a key while the server was still migrating

`.github/workflows/ci.yml:41` ran `geomcp-admin keys create` right after `docker run -d`, so two processes could run the first Alembic migration on the same SQLite file at once.
Fixed in bce259b: the key is created after `/healthz` answers.

### L8. Jobs are cleaned up only at start-up

`packages/server/src/geomcp_server/jobs.py:47` deletes jobs older than seven days when the runner starts, so a long-running container accumulates rows.
Open: rows are small; a periodic cleanup is not worth the code today.

### L9. Large cassettes

CORINE 2.3 MB, traffic 1.7 MB, Lubin 1.2 MB, 10 MB in total, mostly inherent (national CSVs, map tiles, polygons).
Open: acceptable for now; CORINE could shrink with a smaller test area if it bothers the owner.

### L10. The `date` parameter of `get_snapshot` shadows the `date` type inside the function

`packages/server/src/geomcp_server/app.py:423`: harmless today, but easy to trip over.
Open: renaming changes the tool contract, so it is the owner's call.

### L11. Ground motion statistics use the convex hull

`packages/core/src/geomcp_core/constraints.py:202` sends the convex hull to EGMS, so a concave gmina includes some ground outside it.
Open: the result is already labelled interpolated and an indication; a ring with holes would need the multipolygon handling the service may not accept.

## What was checked and found sound

- API keys are a 256-bit secret hashed with SHA-256, compared in constant time, revocable and shown once.
  Malformed and unknown tokens are rejected (`auth.py`, `test_auth.py`).
- Google sign-in checks the email and `email_verified` claims on every token through `load_access_token`.
  An `@domain` entry matches only that domain, the server refuses to start without an allowlist, and the Google user's email replaces the client id in logs and stats.
- `/metrics` needs a key through the same verifier chain.
  `/files` links are HMAC-signed over name and expiry, expire after 24 hours, and the name regex excludes path traversal.
  `/healthz` exposes only the version.
- `.env` is ignored by git and Docker.
  No personal path, owner email or secret was found in docs, cassettes or `TOOL_EXAMPLES.md`.
  Notary names and deed numbers are redacted in the RCN cassette and dropped at parse time.
- EPSG:2180 axis order is right in every service.
  RCN uses northing first with the URN CRS (verified in Kraków by the owner), BDOT10k easting first (checked at Kąty Wrocławskie), GUGiK NMT easting first, KIMPZP and KIEG northing first in WMS 1.3.0 (the Kazimierz cassette is off the Wrocław diagonal).
- Prices use free-market single-property sales only, PLN per square metre of floor for apartments and of land for plots, quartiles from `statistics.quantiles`, and omit years with under three sales.
- Noise rendering paints the band's lower bound into the red channel with anti-aliasing off, with shares at 55, 65 and 70 dB per source.
- GIOŚ history drops years under 75 percent complete, keeps the most complete series per year, and picks the nearest station with a current series of at least five years.
- The GHSL, PVGIS, CORINE and Open-Meteo reads have correct tile arithmetic, window reads, clipping to the real polygon and model completeness checks.
- Retries happen only on 429, 5xx and transport errors, with `source_busy` and `source_forbidden` mapping, GUS BDL long-wait handling, CEIDG 3.7 second pacing, Nominatim at one request per second and an Overpass mirror fallback.
- Docker is a multi-stage build with a non-root user and a health check.
  `docker compose build` succeeds, and the container answers `/healthz`, refuses `/mcp` and `/metrics` without a key, accepts a key, initialises a session and answers `resolve_area`.

## Open questions for the owner

- Q1. Landslides: PIG-PIB's SOPO layers refuse the query operation, so landslides are a reported gap now; which access path should replace it (WMS GetFeatureInfo on the SOPO service, the SOPO viewer's API, or the Geoportal integration)? Adding one is a new source and was out of scope.
- Q2. Should Google sign-in restrict client redirect URIs to the clients the team uses (claude.ai, Claude Desktop, Claude Code callbacks)? Today any registered client may name any redirect URI; the consent page is the guard.
- Q3. Which contact should the User-Agent carry for Nominatim and the other public services (a repository URL or an email)?
- Q4. Should a settled weather period with missing days be reported as `preliminary` rather than `final` with a lower `valid_fraction`?
- Q5. Should the ISOK result keep listing the four flood scenarios when a scenario fails, or report the failed scenario as a gap as it does now? The current behaviour follows the "gap over wrong number" rule.
- Q6. Should FastMCP's `auto` Host guard be enabled for loopback binds (local `geomcp serve --http` runs), which would reject a forwarded public Host unless `GEOMCP_ALLOWED_HOSTS` names it? The review left it off so that nothing changes without the setting.

## After the merge

- Rebuild on the VPS: `docker compose up -d --build`.
- No new `.env` setting is required; if `GEOMCP_ALLOWED_HOSTS` is set it is enforced from now on, so make sure nginx passes `Host $host` (the shipped config does) and that the list names the public host.
- Cached constraints and traffic results recompute once (their cache keys changed); Sentinel Hub chunks are reused.
- MCP clients need no reconnect: the tool names, parameters and schemas are unchanged.
- `geomcp-admin stats` shows units on the right row from now on; older rows keep the shifted attribution.
